Making a catalog
1. Pick products — tick them in the list, type SKUs in Arrange & edit, or import a marked-up price list with Import file.
2. Arrange & edit — drag products and whole sections into order, rename anything, add notes, change a photo.
3. Generate PDF — in the print dialog choose Save as PDF, set Margins: None and tick Background graphics, or the branding drops out.
Your catalogs are kept under Recent catalogs.
New
A product that isn't in any list yet — in Arrange & edit press + New, or type NEW in the SKU box, and fill in the name, pack size and photo yourself. Typing a SKU nobody has offers the same thing.
Prices, if you want them — tick Show prices in Details. Importing a price list fills them in for you, Edit has a price box for the rest, and anything without a price simply prints without one.
